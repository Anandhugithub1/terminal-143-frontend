Tinder – Most Popular, Mostly Casual

Tinder is the most widely used dating app in Thailand, especially in Bangkok, Phuket, and Pattaya. The massive user base means you'll find plenty of matches, but quality varies significantly. Great for casual dating and meeting new people, but less reliable for serious relationships due to fake profiles and ghosting.

Huge user base
Many fake/inactive profiles
Easy to use
Very casual focus

ThaiFriendly – Local Favorite, Mixed Quality

ThaiFriendly has been around for years and is very popular among Thai locals. Free messaging is a big plus, but the platform suffers from spam accounts, freelancers, and low-effort profiles. It can work for casual dating if you're patient and careful, but not recommended for serious relationship seekers.

Heads up: Many experienced users report high numbers of fake or commercial profiles. Always verify before meeting.

Bumble & Other Apps

Bumble is growing in Bangkok and among educated, English-speaking Thais. Women make the first move, which reduces spam. Other apps like Coffee Meets Bagel and Hinge have smaller user bases but attract more serious daters.

  • Bumble – quality matches, smaller pool
  • Hinge – very limited in Thailand
  • Facebook Dating – free but mixed quality
