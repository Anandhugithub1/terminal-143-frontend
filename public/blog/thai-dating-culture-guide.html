Dating in Thailand explained

Thai dating culture focuses on respect, patience, and long-term potential rather than casual encounters.

Key cultural differences

  • Indirect communication style
  • Respect is very important
  • Family involvement in serious relationships

Do’s and don’ts

  • Be polite and calm
  • Don’t rush intimacy
  • Avoid being overly aggressive
